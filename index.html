HanjaPath Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

HanjaPath does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All app data — study progress, saved characters, streak counts, quiz scores, and theme preferences — is stored locally on your device only. No data leaves your device.

No accounts. No analytics. No ads. No tracking.

The HanziWriter animation library is loaded from jsDelivr CDN for the character writing screens. No user data is sent to jsDelivr.

To delete all data, uninstall the app.
